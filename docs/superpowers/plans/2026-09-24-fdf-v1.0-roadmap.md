# FDF 1.0 — delivery roadmap

**Spec:** [2026-09-24-fdf-v1.0-design.md](../specs/2026-09-24-fdf-v1.0-design.md)

The 1.0 design spans five subsystems that depend on each other in order: the
foundations, the validator, the commands, `fdf migrate`, and the install,
skills and documentation. It is delivered as five plans, each written in full
once the one before it has landed, because each plan builds on the exact APIs
its predecessor creates.

The plans **expand, then contract**. Plans 1 to 4 add 1.0 beside 0.x, so every
step leaves the tests green; Plan 5 removes 0.x and ships. No release is cut
from the integration branch until Plan 5 is done.

## Branches

- **`v1`** is the integration branch, created from `claude/v1-0-design` (which
  holds the design, this roadmap and the plans) once they are reviewed.
- **Each plan** is worked on a branch off `v1` and merged into `v1` by pull
  request, once CI passes (`go vet ./...`, `go test ./...`, `gofmt -l .`
  printing nothing, and the skills' frontmatter lint).
- **0.7.1** fixes `fdf mv` for everyone still on 0.7, and is released from
  `main`. It is Plan 1's Tasks 3 and 4, the `links` engine and `fdf mv` on it,
  applied to a branch off `main`. Those tasks touch nothing 1.0 changes, and
  they fix three defects in 0.7.0:
  - links that leave the bundle break on a move that changes depth;
  - a footnote's first word is rewritten as a path;
  - a reference definition inside a code fence is rewritten.

  When 0.7.1 lands, merge `main` into `v1`; Plan 1 then skips the two tasks
  already done.
- **When Plan 5 is done**, `v1` merges into `main` and `v1.0.0` is tagged
  there.

## The plans

| # | Plan | Delivers | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Foundations](2026-09-24-fdf-v1.0-plan-1-foundations.md) | `specver` (MAJOR.MINOR versions, compared by number); `links`, the one link-repair engine; `fdf mv` on the engine, which fixes its broken links on moves that change depth | — |
| 2 | Validator learns 1.0 | `spec/1.0.md`; the `layout` package; `1.0` among the supported pins; for a 1.0 pin, positions from `layout` (the closed root, `features/`, nested groups, flat features, the directory rule), the did-you-mean hint (F10, F14), `features` among F12's register names, and links read by `links.Find`; the 1.0 fixtures. 0.x validation unchanged | 1 |
| 3 | Commands write 1.0 | `CurrentVersion` 1.0 and `fdf init`; root resolution (`docs/fdf/`, and also `docs/features/`); `new`, `adopt`, `practice`, `debt`, `bug`, `change`, `fix` on `layout`, with nested groups and name clashes refused; full feature IDs in `--affects` and `history`; `mv`, `lexicon`, `log`, `release`, the registers' `--cleanup` and `adopt`'s map on `layout`; `help` and `spec`. Commands point a 0.x bundle at `fdf migrate` | 2 |
| 4 | `fdf migrate` 0.x → 1.0 | Design §6 in full: the plan computed first, `--dry-run`, feature groups into `features/`, IDs, links, indexes, pin, relocation and `--to`, references outside the bundle, the refusals, the log, the report and the next steps | 1, 2, 3 |
| 5 | Contract and ship | The validator drops 0.x (version gates removed; the 0.x fixtures converted with migrate or removed; the pin required; the soft-check changes); `fdf install` (the primer, `legacyPrimers`, recognizing the root recorded in `.fdf-version`); the ten skills; `README.md`, `SPEC.md`, `spec/README.md`, this repository's `CLAUDE.md` and `embed.go`; acceptance on a scratch clone of ginee; release 1.0.0 | 4 |
